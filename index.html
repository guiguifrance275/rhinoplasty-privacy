FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the Live Overlay feature?

Import a reference photo in the Overlay tab. The app will overlay it on your live camera feed. Use the sliders to adjust opacity and blend mode.

My photos are not saving. What should I do?

Make sure you have granted the app permission to access your Photo Library. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos and enable access for Rhinoplasty Simulator Pro.

How do I export a Before/After video?

Load two videos in the Before/After tab, adjust the framing with your fingers, then tap "Crop and Export Video". You can trim the video and add your logo watermark before the final export.

Is my patient data stored on your servers?

No. All data stays on your device. Patient photos and records are stored locally and never transmitted to any external server.

The camera opens the photo library instead. How do I fix this?

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and make sure Rhinoplasty Simulator Pro is enabled.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Rhinoplasty Simulator Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All photos, patient records, and simulations remain on your device at all times.

The app requests access to your Camera and Photo Library solely to enable simulation and comparison features. This data is never shared with third parties.
