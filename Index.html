Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your privacy is fundamental to us. Rhinoplasty Simulator does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data or patient photos with external servers. All data remains on your device.

1. Who We Are

Rhinoplasty Simulator Pro and Rhinoplasty Simulator Lite are applications published for iOS and iPadOS. They are designed for plastic surgeons, ENT specialists, and patients interested in rhinoplasty simulation.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required
  • No photos or patient data are transmitted to our servers
  • No analytics or tracking tools are embedded in the app
  • No advertising identifiers are used

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Patient photos and simulation results (stored in the app's Documents folder)
  • Patient folder names and metadata (stored in your device's local storage)
  • App settings and preferences

If you enable iCloud sync, this data is synced via your personal Apple iCloud account. We have no access to your iCloud data.

4. iCloud Synchronisation

Rhinoplasty Simulator Pro offers optional iCloud Documents synchronisation to sync patient data between your iPhone and iPad. This sync uses Apple's iCloud infrastructure exclusively. No data passes through our servers.

5. In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to all transactions.

6. Camera and Photo Library

The app requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to import photos for simulation. Photos are never transmitted outside your device without your explicit action (export, share).

7. Patient Consent & GDPR

The use of this application by healthcare professionals implies that the practitioner has obtained the patient's informed consent for taking, processing and storing photos in compliance with GDPR (EU) and applicable local regulations. The practitioner is solely responsible for data security (device lock, backup, access control).

8. Medical Disclaimer

Rhinoplasty Simulator is not a medical device. Simulations produced by this application do not constitute a medical diagnosis, a guaranteed surgical outcome, or a substitute for professional medical advice. Results shown are illustrative only.

9. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adults and healthcare professionals only. We do not knowingly collect any data from persons under the age of 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
